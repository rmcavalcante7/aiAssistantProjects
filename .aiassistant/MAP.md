## Vault Scope

This vault is `.aiassistant`.

Repository-root files such as `../README.md` and `../AGENTS.md` are outside this vault and do not appear as Obsidian graph nodes when `.aiassistant` is opened directly.

Keep those files in the repository root because they are entrypoints for humans and agents operating from the project root.
